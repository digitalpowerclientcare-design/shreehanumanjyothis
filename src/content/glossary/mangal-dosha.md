---
term: Mangal Dosha
definition: Mangal Dosha is a chart condition in which Mars occupies the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house from a given reference point, subject to several recognised cancellation conditions.
alsoKnownAs: ["Manglik", "Kuja Dosham", "Chevvai Dosham"]
relatedService: mangal-dosha
