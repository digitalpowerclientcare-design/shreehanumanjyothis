## How the recommendation is made

The chart is read first: planetary strength and placement, the running dasha, and which planet — if any — a remedial stone would be supporting. Only then is a stone considered.

You are told the stone, an appropriate weight range, the metal, the finger, and the day to begin.

## Why we do not sell stones

Because the recommendation should not be influenced by what is in stock. It is the most common conflict of interest in this field, and the simplest way to remove it is not to be in that business.

Buy from a jeweller you trust, ask for certification on anything expensive, and do not feel pressured into a larger stone than the recommendation specifies.

## When none is indicated

This is a common outcome. If your chart does not call for a stone, you will be told so, and the consultation ends there without a sale.
