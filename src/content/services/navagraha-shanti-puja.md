## What the observance addresses

The Navagraha — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu. The observance is directed towards the graha or graha indicated by the chart, rather than being a single fixed procedure applied to everyone.

## When it is suggested

Where a chart reading shows a specific planetary period or placement that the tradition addresses through this observance. It follows a reading; it is not offered before one.

## What is explained beforehand

What the observance involves, how long it takes, what materials are needed, what it is traditionally understood to address, and what it is not claimed to do. If you would rather not proceed, that is entirely fine and it does not change the guidance you have already received.

## Arranging a date

Dates are chosen with reference to [muhurtha](/services/muhurtha/). Observances can be arranged at any of our three centres, in Bengaluru, Hyderabad or Mumbai.
