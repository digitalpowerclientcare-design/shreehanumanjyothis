## What is covered

**Launch and expansion timing** — dasha periods and transits indicating supported windows.
**Partnership assessment** — reading partners' charts together for working style and compatibility.
**Business name analysis** — traditional numerological consideration, offered as an input rather than a prescription.
**Difficult periods** — what the chart indicates about the phase and its likely duration.
**Combined with Vastu** — many owners take chart guidance and [premises Vastu](/services/vastu-for-office/) together.

## The limits, stated plainly

A birth chart does not determine commercial outcomes. Market conditions, capital, product, execution and competition do. What a chart can offer is a view on timing and on the temperament of the people involved.

We do not give investment advice, we do not advise on specific financial products, and we will not tell you that a remedy will improve your results.
