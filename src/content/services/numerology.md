## What it covers

**Birth number**, derived from the day of the month. **Destiny number**, derived from the full date of birth. **Name analysis**, from the numerical values assigned to the letters of a name as currently used.

## How we treat it

As one input, weighted below the birth chart. Where a numerological indication and a chart indication point differently, we go with the chart and say so.

## What we will not tell you

That a name change will transform your circumstances. That a particular number is responsible for your difficulties. That an expensive correction is required. Those claims are common in this field and they do not stand up.
