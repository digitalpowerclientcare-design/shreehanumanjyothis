## When people use it

- **Birth time unknown or unreliable** — extremely common, and the usual reason someone is turned away elsewhere
- **One urgent decision** — an offer to accept or decline, a date to commit to, a matter to pursue or drop
- **A first consultation** — some people prefer to start with one question before a full reading
- **Someone else's matter** — where you cannot obtain another person's birth details

## How it works

You ask one clear question. A chart is cast for that moment and that place. The reading works from the lagna at that instant, the position of the significators for the matter in question, and the relationships between them.

The answer comes back as a direction and, where the chart supports it, an indication of timing — usually as a period rather than a date.

## What to expect

A Prasna reading is short and focused. It will not cover your career, your marriage and your health in one sitting; it will address the question you asked. If the chart is unclear on the matter — which happens — you will be told that rather than given a confident answer the chart does not support.

If you do have your birth details, a full [Kundli reading](/services/kundli-reading/) covers considerably more ground.
