## Where this helps

**Before a commitment.** Reading both charts together gives you compatibility, temperament and timing before a decision gets harder to unwind.

**During family disagreement.** A formal reading gives both sides something concrete to discuss rather than talking past each other. It carries weight in many families.

**During a difficult phase.** When a relationship has gone quiet or hard, the chart often shows whether the pressure is coming from a passing dasha period or from something more fundamental.

**After a separation.** Not to reverse it. To understand what happened, what your own chart indicates about the period you are in, and what to do next.

## What we will not do

This is worth stating plainly, because the market around this service is full of claims that do not hold.

- We do not guarantee that a specific person will return or agree to marry.
- We do not offer vashikaran or any practice framed as influencing another person's will.
- We do not sell rituals as a condition for an outcome.
- We do not use fear — about curses, black magic, or doshas — to move you towards a purchase.

If you have been told any of those things by someone else, that is worth weighing carefully.

## How the consultation runs

Send your birth details — and your partner's, if you have them — on WhatsApp. The charts are prepared beforehand. The consultation is a conversation: what the charts show, what the current period indicates, and what is genuinely open to you. Where the honest answer is that the chart does not support what you were hoping for, you will be told that.
