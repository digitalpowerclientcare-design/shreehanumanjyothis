## What is examined

The seventh house and its lord; the position and strength of Venus and Jupiter; Mars placements relevant to the seventh and eighth; the running dasha and antardasha; and the current transits of Jupiter and Saturn across the relevant houses.

## What the answer looks like

An indicative window — for example, that the period from a certain dasha change onward is more supportive than the current one — together with an explanation of which factor is producing the delay.

For many people the useful finding is simply that nothing in the chart is obstructed, and the supportive period has not yet arrived. That is a common and unremarkable result, and it deserves to be reported plainly rather than dressed up as a problem requiring intervention.

## A note on how this subject is usually sold

Marriage delay is one of the most heavily exploited concerns in this market, particularly where a daughter's marriage is involved. Families are told about severe doshas, warned about consequences, and quoted large sums for remedies. If that has happened to you, get a second reading before you spend anything.
