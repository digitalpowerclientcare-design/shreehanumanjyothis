## Who this is for

- **Families outside our three cities** — most of India is a long way from Sadashiva Nagar, Kukatpally or Prabhadevi
- **Relatives abroad** who need to be part of an alliance discussion happening in India
- **Anyone comparing two horoscopes** where the families live in different cities
- **People who would rather not be seen** walking into an astrology centre — a real and reasonable concern

## What stays exactly the same

The chart is prepared the same way from the same three details. The reading covers the same ground. Remedies are suggested on the same basis — only where the chart indicates them, always with the reasoning explained.

Fees are the same as an in-person consultation. There is no online surcharge and no "urgent reading" premium.

## What is different

You do not get the room. For some people that matters, particularly for a first consultation about a family matter, and if it matters to you the centres are there.

**Palmistry cannot be done online.** We would rather say that than take a fee for a photograph reading we do not think is sound.

## Booking across time zones

Tell us your city and time zone when you message. Appointments are arranged around your working day, not ours — a consultation squeezed into a 3am slot is not a consultation anybody benefits from.

## A note on how online astrology is usually sold

Much of what is marketed as "online astrology" is an app subscription, a per-minute chat queue, or an automated report with a human name attached. This is none of those. It is a scheduled appointment with one astrologer who has read your chart beforehand, at a fee agreed in advance.
