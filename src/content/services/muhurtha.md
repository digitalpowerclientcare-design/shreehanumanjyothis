## What a muhurtha is calculated from

The five Panchanga elements — **tithi**, **vara**, **nakshatra**, **yoga** and **karana** — together with the lagna at the proposed time, the position of the Moon, and the avoidance of inauspicious periods such as Rahu Kalam. That selection is then checked against the birth charts of the people directly involved.

## Common occasions

| Occasion | Typical notice |
| --- | --- |
| Marriage | 2–3 months |
| Gruhapravesham (housewarming) | 3–4 weeks |
| Business or shop opening | 3–4 weeks |
| Vehicle purchase | A few days |
| Starting construction or laying a foundation | 3–4 weeks |
| Beginning education, or a naming ceremony | 2–3 weeks |

## What you receive

One or more suitable dates with a specific time window for each, the reasoning behind the selection, and any periods within the day to avoid. If none of the suggested dates work practically, tell us and we will look again — a muhurtha you cannot actually use is of no value.
