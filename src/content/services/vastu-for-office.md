## What is assessed

Main entrance direction and approach · owner and management seating · staff seating orientation · accounts, cash and safe placement · reception and waiting area · storage and heavy equipment · pantry and washrooms · server and electrical rooms · for retail, the display and billing layout.

## Types of premises

**Offices** — cabin allocation, seating direction, meeting rooms, reception.
**Retail** — entrance, billing counter, display orientation, stockroom.
**Workshops and small industry** — machinery placement, raw material and finished goods storage, fire and water zones.
**Clinics and consulting rooms** — practitioner seating, waiting area, records.

## Working within constraints

Most commercial premises are leased, fitted out, and occupied. Recommendations are given in priority order and separated into what can be done immediately, what to plan for at the next refit, and what would only apply to a new premises.
