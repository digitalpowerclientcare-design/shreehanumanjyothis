## What the chart shows

A Janam Kundli maps the position of the planets at the moment and place of your birth. From it we read:

- **The lagna and house structure** — the framework the rest of the chart sits in
- **Planetary placements and their strength** in each house
- **Yogas** — specific planetary combinations with recognised significance
- **Dasha and antardasha** — the periods that indicate when patterns are most active
- **Transits** — where the current planetary movements meet your chart

## What details are needed

Three things: **date of birth**, **time of birth** (as accurate as possible), and **place of birth**. Send them on WhatsApp before your appointment and the chart will be ready when you arrive.

## How the consultation runs

The chart is prepared in advance. During the consultation we walk through what it shows in the areas you have asked about, using plain language rather than technical vocabulary — though if you want the technical detail, ask and you will get it.

Where the chart indicates a difficult period, we say so and explain what the indication actually covers. Where a remedy is genuinely appropriate, it is suggested with its reasoning. Where nothing is required, we say that too.

## Timing questions

Most people come with a timing question — when to change jobs, when a marriage is likely, whether this is the right year for a property decision. Vedic astrology approaches these through dasha periods and transits, and it does so in ranges rather than dates. Anyone offering you an exact date for an uncertain future event is overstating what the method can do.
