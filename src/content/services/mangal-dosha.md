## What it is

Mangal Dosha — also called Kuja Dosham or Chevvai Dosham — refers to Mars occupying the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house, counted from the lagna, the Moon, or Venus depending on the tradition being followed.

## Why it is so often over-stated

Three reasons, all worth knowing:

1. **Cancellation conditions are widely ignored.** Classical texts list many circumstances in which the dosha is nullified or substantially reduced — the sign Mars occupies, aspects from Jupiter, the age of the person, and whether both charts carry it, among others.
2. **Reference points differ between traditions.** A chart can be manglik from the lagna but not from the Moon. Software rarely tells you which reference it used.
3. **It is commercially useful to whoever is telling you.** A frightened family is an easier sale than a reassured one.

## How we assess it

We check whether the placement exists, from which reference points, how strong and well-placed Mars actually is, which aspects fall on it, and which cancellation conditions apply. You are told the finding and its actual weight — including, frequently, that it carries very little.

Where it does carry weight, that is said plainly too, and it is factored into the [compatibility reading](/services/kundli-matching/) rather than treated as a standalone verdict.
